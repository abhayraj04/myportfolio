Case Study 1 • Window Functions & Aggregation

Customer Retention & Monthly Revenue at Risk

Business Question: Which contract cohorts contribute the highest churn rate and total lost monthly revenue, and how do they rank within each payment category?

MySQL / PostgreSQL Query 
-- Analyze churn rate and revenue at risk by contract and payment method
SELECT 
    contract_type,
    payment_method,
    COUNT(customer_id) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn_status = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN churn_status = 'Yes' THEN 1.0 ELSE 0.0 END) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate_pct,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN churn_status = 'Yes' THEN monthly_charges ELSE 0 END), 2) AS monthly_revenue_at_risk,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (
        PARTITION BY payment_method 
        ORDER BY SUM(CASE WHEN churn_status = 'Yes' THEN monthly_charges ELSE 0 END) DESC
    ) AS risk_rank_in_payment_group
FROM telecom_customers
GROUP BY contract_type, payment_method
HAVING COUNT(customer_id) >= 50
ORDER BY monthly_revenue_at_risk DESC;
Key Analytical Insight: Month-to-month contracts paying via electronic check accounted for over 55% of total revenue at risk, highlighting priority targets for quarterly retention incentives.
Case Study 2 • Multi-Table Joins & Filtering

Department Placement Rates & Eligibility Gating

Business Question: For each academic department, what proportion of eligible students (CGPA ≥ 7.0 with 0 active backlogs) have secured at least one placement offer?

MySQL Query 
-- Calculate placement conversion rates for eligible engineering candidates
SELECT 
    d.department_name,
    COUNT(s.student_id) AS eligible_candidates,
    COUNT(DISTINCT p.student_id) AS placed_students,
    ROUND((COUNT(DISTINCT p.student_id) * 100.0) / NULLIF(COUNT(s.student_id), 0), 2) AS placement_rate_pct,
    ROUND(AVG(p.annual_ctc_lpa), 2) AS avg_package_lpa
FROM departments d
INNER JOIN students s 
    ON d.department_id = s.department_id
LEFT JOIN placement_offers p 
    ON s.student_id = p.student_id
WHERE s.cgpa >= 7.00 
  AND s.active_backlogs = 0
GROUP BY d.department_id, d.department_name
ORDER BY placement_rate_pct DESC;
Key Analytical Insight: Computer Science and Electronics had the highest eligibility-to-offer conversion (>80%), while highlighting students requiring targeted interview preparation.

Customer Churn Classifier

Classification Metrics & Trade-Offs

Because customer churn datasets frequently exhibit class imbalance (non-churners outnumber churners ~3:1), high accuracy can be misleading. Performance is therefore evaluated primarily via ROC-AUC and Recall.

Model ROC-AUC Precision (Churn) Recall (Churn) F1-Score
Random Forest + Tuning 0.84 0.68 0.79 0.73
XGBoost Baseline 0.82 0.69 0.74 0.71
Logistic Regression 0.79 0.62 0.71 0.66

Top Contributing Factors (via SHAP):

1. Tenure (Months) • Negative Corr. 2. Month-to-Month Contract • Positive Corr. 3. Monthly Charges • Positive Corr. 4. Tech Support Enrollment • Negative Corr.
Acknowledged Limitations: The model prioritizes recall to catch at-risk customers early, which accepts a moderate false-positive rate. In production, retention outreach budgets must guide threshold calibration.
